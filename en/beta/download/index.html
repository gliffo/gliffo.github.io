On Mac

“Apple cannot verify…”

This Beta is not yet signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple. If you downloaded it from the Gliffo website and want to allow it to open:

  1. Open the DMG and try to launch Gliffo Suite Beta.app. Dismiss the warning.
  2. Go to the Apple menu → System Settings → Privacy & Security.
  3. In the Security section, find the Gliffo warning and click Open Anyway.
  4. Authorize with Touch ID or your Mac password if requested, then confirm Open. In the installer, choose Install (Installa).

“Open Anyway” appears after you try to open the app and remains available for about an hour. If it is missing, try opening the app again and return to Settings. A company-managed Mac may require an administrator. On earlier macOS versions, go to System Preferences → Security & Privacy → General.

You do not need to disable Gatekeeper or use Terminal. The Adobe signature on the extension is separate from the Apple signature on the installer.

On Windows

“Windows protected your PC”

The Beta EXE does not yet have an Authenticode signature. SmartScreen may show a reputation or unknown-publisher warning.

  1. Check that you downloaded Gliffo Suite Beta.exe from the Gliffo website.
  2. Click More info and check the filename.
  3. If you recognize the file and want to run it, click Run anyway, when available.

If the button is missing, company policies or Smart App Control may be active: contact your administrator or report the warning. Do not disable SmartScreen, Smart App Control or your antivirus.

In Illustrator

The Suite is missing from the menu
  1. Make sure you are using Adobe Illustrator 2024 or later.
  2. Quit Illustrator completely and reopen it after installation.
  3. Look for Window → Extensions → Gliffo Suite PRO. The menu may be called Extensions (Legacy), depending on your version.

If it is still missing, send a report with your operating system and Illustrator version.

The installer asks me to close Illustrator

Closing a document or window does not quit the application.

  • Mac: choose Illustrator → Quit Illustrator, or press ⌘Q.
  • Windows: save your work and close all Illustrator windows.

Wait for the application to quit, then restart the installer.

Malware or damaged-file warning?

If the warning mentions malware, says the file “will damage your computer”, or says it “is damaged”, do not force it to open. Send us the message or a screenshot. If the download is incomplete, download it again from the website.
