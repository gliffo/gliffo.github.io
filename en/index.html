Before you start.

How do I use the Suite?

Install the Beta with Illustrator closed. Then open Window → Extensions → Gliffo Suite PRO.

What happens after 15 days?

The trial ends. Your files remain usable and you can still save your project.

Are projects saved online?

No. Projects and assets stay in a folder on your computer.
