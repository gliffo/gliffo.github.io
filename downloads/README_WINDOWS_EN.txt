GLIFFO SUITE BETA — INSTALLATION

Free Beta: 15 days from the first launch. Adobe Illustrator 2024 or later.
The Suite interface is available in Italian and English. It follows Illustrator’s UI language; override it with “Lingua / Language” on the home panel. The installer is currently in Italian.
Save your work and quit Illustrator before installing or uninstalling.

1. Download Gliffo Suite Beta.exe from the official Gliffo website.
2. Open the EXE and follow the installation steps.
3. Reopen Illustrator after installation.

If SmartScreen displays Windows protected your PC:
Check the source and filename. Click More info, then Run anyway if available and you intend to run the installer. The Beta EXE does not yet have an Authenticode signature. If company policies or Smart App Control block it, contact your administrator. Do not disable SmartScreen, Smart App Control or antivirus protection.

Open the Suite in Illustrator: Window > Extensions > Gliffo Suite PRO. The menu may be called Extensions (Legacy). In the Italian interface: Finestra > Estensioni.

If a warning says the file is malware, will damage your computer or is damaged, do not force it to open. Contact us with the exact message. If the download is incomplete, download it again.
