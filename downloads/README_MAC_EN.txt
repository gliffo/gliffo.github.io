GLIFFO SUITE BETA — INSTALLATION

Free Beta: 15 days from the first launch. Adobe Illustrator 2024 or later.
The app and installer interface are currently in Italian.
Save your work and quit Illustrator before installing or uninstalling.

1. Download Gliffo Suite Beta.dmg from the official Gliffo website.
2. Open the DMG and launch Gliffo Suite Beta.app.
3. Choose Install (Installa), then reopen Illustrator.

If macOS shows an unknown-developer or Apple-cannot-verify warning:
Try opening the app once and dismiss the warning. Go to Apple menu > System Settings > Privacy & Security. Find the Gliffo warning and choose Open Anyway, then authorize and confirm Open. A company-managed Mac may require an administrator.
The Beta installer is not yet signed with an Apple Developer ID or notarized. The Adobe signature on the extension is separate from Apple code signing. Do not disable Gatekeeper or SIP.

To uninstall, reopen the installer and choose Uninstall (Disinstalla). Saved projects and the trial start date are retained.

Open the Suite in Illustrator: Window > Extensions > Gliffo Suite PRO. The menu may be called Extensions (Legacy). In the Italian interface: Finestra > Estensioni.

If a warning says the file is malware, will damage your computer or is damaged, do not force it to open. Contact us with the exact message. If the download is incomplete, download it again.
